# floodfusion v1.3 — decisions and deviations log

This log records, **before the result it could affect exists**, every rule, decision and disclosure that bears on
v1.3's claims (`reports/protocol_v1_3.md`). Dates are UTC; one row per line.

## Disclosures

| Date (UTC) | Disclosure |
|---|---|
| 2026-11-10 | v1.3 was designed with v1.2's results on the seen CEMS benchmark known (row 21: 0.417 at τ*, 0.468 at the oracle threshold, `reports/v12_cems_rebuilt.md`). The ladder's rungs were chosen because of them. Every v1.3 number on that benchmark is therefore exploratory; only H8 is confirmatory. |
| 2026-11-10 | Four of the 30 clusters (v1.1's definition: activation extents within 100 km and 60 days) span two folds of the ladder: EMSR755 (with EMSR756, EMSR761, EMSR763), EMSR789 (with EMSR796), EMSR850 (with EMSR854, EMSR856) and EMSR861 (with EMSR864). The ladder's groups link the stacks' own footprints, as the v1.2 pool does (protocol §2.1), and those activations' mapped tiles lie more than 100 km apart. So a fold model can be trained on one activation of a storm and tested on another activation of the same storm, in a different place. The rule is per activation, and each activation sits in one fold. Found when the descriptive per-cluster table refused to run, before any rung-2 number was computed. |
| 2026-11-11 | The paper's Figure 1, the per-activation association between v1.2's IoU at τ* and the reference ceiling (Spearman ρ = 0.51 over the 27 activations in both reports), was chosen after both had been computed. No rule was set for it in advance: it is exploratory and labelled so in the paper (`paper/igarss27/make_figures.py`). |

| 2026-11-10 | **The per-cluster table (descriptive):** a cluster's IoU pools its activations' pixel counts, each activation cut at its own fold's threshold. | Clusters can span folds (disclosure above); one threshold per cluster would borrow a fold's threshold for an activation it held out. Before any rung-2 number. |
| 2026-11-10 | **The reference ceiling's set, made exact:** consecutive products are paired among the **delivered** delineations only, those with a download path. 221 of the 883 dated DEL products of the seen flood activations have none (an AOI product never made, e.g. marked not feasible). The set is therefore 80 pairs in 34 activations; the metadata probe's 111 in 45 had counted undelivered products. One package of the 80 now returns HTTP 404 (EMSR683 AOI09 MONIT01), so its pair is listed as not compared. | An undelivered product is not a map. Found when the download step ran; no product was compared and no IoU computed. |
| 2026-11-10 | **Rung 3's runs, fixed before any of them started.**<br>**Tuning:** `floodfusion.tuning` on fold 0's inner split of `data/pool_v13` with `--grid 1e-5 3e-5 1e-4`. Every rate's run starts from `runs/v12/final/all/all/row21_fusion_context_seasonal/seed42` (`train --init-from`: its `best.pt` and its normalisation statistics) with the Kuro Siwo overlay. The chosen rate goes to `reports/v13_lr_row21_fusion_context_seasonal.yaml`, which replaces v1.2's learning-rate overlay.<br>**Folds:** five, seed 42, from the same start.<br>**Scoring:** each fold model scores, with the v1.2 scorer (`cems_scores_v12.score`), only the pairs of the activations its fold holds out (every stratum; the rule reads the primary one), at its own τ*.<br>**Lock:** the queue takes the GPU lock per run, so a blind-test sweep can take the GPU between runs. | Protocol §3 names the recipe; this records how it is run. |
| 2026-11-10 | **Stacks without a seasonal reference:** 8 of the 517 stacks (930 of 30,834 chips, EMSR848 AOI01 among them) have no `seasonal.tif`. Each has a `seasonal.json` recording why: fewer than 3 same-orbit acquisitions in the window. Training reads them as seasonal-unavailable (zeros and the availability flag), as the v1.2 scorer already does. v1.2's 1 % rule for the pool guarded against an unfinished data chain; here every missing sidecar has a recorded reason. | Before any rung-3 run. |
| 2026-11-10 | **Rung 3's cost, disclosed:** row 21's sampler draws every positive chip and three negatives per positive. On a fold of `data/pool_v13` that is all 18.5k training chips per epoch (v1.2: 4.2k), about 290 s per epoch on this GPU. Rung 3 therefore costs about 20–28 GPU-hours, above the plan's 12–18. The recipe is not shortened to save time. | Before any rung-3 run. |
| 2026-11-10 | **The reference ceiling's sensitivity row:** the macro IoU is also reported without the pairs whose later product repeats more than 5 % of its flood area from images no later than the earlier product's (the carry-over probe). Those are EMSR657 AOI01's three pairs (43–58 %); every other pair repeats none. | Two maps that share polygons agree by construction. Fixed from the probe's shares alone, before any IoU was computed. |
| 2026-11-11 | **The live service before the switch (protocol §6, made exact).** Until `blind_floods` in `configs/live.yaml` is switched on, a commit made only after `v1.3.0-freeze` (or after the ladder decides that there is no v1.3 model) and on the author's go:<br>**Codes:** the service refuses every activation code from EMSR899 on, before any request for its record.<br>**Dates:** a map refuses a pass date `d` when `d` plus the optical window (2 days) reaches 20 July 2026, the first event date of the blind set (`reports/protocol_v1_2.md`, Corrections). A map of a later date could show a blind flood. A map searches its radar pass on its date only, and optical within ±2 days of it.<br>**Seen activations (EMSR656–898):** the service reads only the record's code, name, category, event date and AOI extents, never a product. The live page shows no Copernicus outline and no score for any flood.<br>**Enforced by** `tests/test_live_rules.py`, which also checks that the committed switch is off. Switching it on edits that test, with a comment naming the go. | Section 6 said *from when* and *what may be read*; it did not say what a map of an arbitrary box and date may show before the switch. A date rule closes that: the ordering rule (v1.3 frozen before any blind output is seen) then holds for manual maps too. |
